DFS terms in plain language
Stack
Your QB plus his own pass catchers, so a big passing day counts twice.
Bring-back
A player from the team your QB plays against: if the game turns into a shootout, both sides score.
Ceiling (p90)
A score the player beats only 1 time in 10. Tournaments are won by ceilings, not averages.
Boom / bust
Boom = a big game for the price (site multiple × salary). Bust = under half of that.
Ownership / chalk
Share of opponents expected to play someone. Chalk = very popular players.
Leverage
Gaining an edge when a player you have and most rivals don't plays well, or vice versa.
Duplication
Other entries with your exact lineup; a win is split with them.
Top 1% / win %
How often a lineup finishes in the top 1% or first across the simulated contests: model estimates.
Expected profit / ROI
Average payout minus the fee across simulations. Only shown with a known fee; estimated when payouts are modeled.
FLEX
The extra slot that can hold an RB, WR or TE.
Late swap
Changing players whose games haven't started yet after earlier games kick off.
Game sim vs contest sim
Game sim = how players score. Contest sim = how your lineups finish against a modeled field.

Load your FanDuel slate

  1. FanDuel: open a main-slate NFL contest, go to the player list and choose Download players list. DraftKings: open a Classic NFL contest and choose Export to CSV under the salaries. The site is detected from the file and the builder switches to it.
  2. Optional: add Proj and Own columns, or upload a separate projections file below. Without them, the site's average points are used and ownership is estimated from value.
  3. Load the file, then paste or enter each game's total and spread on the Vegas lines tab. Players listed as out (O) or on IR are removed automatically.

Projections, ownership & other player data

Files from any provider work. Each player is matched to the official player pool, and its official ID is attached for export. Your file doesn't need IDs. Matching uses the site ID when present, then name, team, position, opponent and salary. Only exact and high-confidence matches are used; anything uncertain goes to Review mapping and is never guessed.

Match your columns

Columns are matched by header meaning (case, spaces and punctuation ignored) and, when a header is unfamiliar, by the values in the column. Fields marked from data were found from the values. Change any that are wrong.

PrizePicks Fantasy Score projections

PrizePicks Fantasy Score lines are a market input, not a DFS projection. They only become a projection if you pick PrizePicks or Blended as the source below, and then they're labeled MODEL ESTIMATE. FanDuel scoring is converted only when a Receptions line exists; otherwise the value stays UNCONVERTED and is not used unless you allow it. DraftKings yardage bonuses are not added. PrizePicks doesn't publish DFS ownership: ownership here is always Estimated. Only standard, pregame, full-game lines are read (no goblin, demon, promo or live lines).

PrizePicks never replaces your CSV unless you pick it here. Edit any player in the Player pool for a custom value.

If auto-import is unavailable, upload or paste the PrizePicks projections data (JSON from PrizePicks, or a CSV with player, team, stat type, line and odds type columns).
